Accounts come from Authentik

OnTrak keeps no password and no user directory. An account is created here the first time someone signs in through the range's Authentik application, keyed on their email address. Membership of the instructor group is what makes someone an instructor, and it is re-read on every sign-in — changing a role below only sticks until that person signs in again.

To take someone off the board, disable them here: a disabled account stays disabled even when they sign in through SSO. Create, rename and offboard people in Authentik.
